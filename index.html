Clip District

Short gaming clips from licensed streamer footage.

Clip District is a small personal tool the Clip District channel uses to publish its own short-form videos. It runs on the channel owner's computer and uploads finished clips to the owner's own TikTok and YouTube accounts after the owner has approved each clip.

It isn't offered to the public. Nobody else can sign in to it, and it doesn't access anyone else's account.
