Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Summary

Clip District runs only on its owner's computer and connects only to the owner's own TikTok and YouTube accounts. It doesn't collect, sell or share data about anyone else.

What data the tool uses

  • Sign-in tokens issued by TikTok and Google when the owner connects their accounts. They are stored only on the owner's computer, in a folder that only the owner can read, and are used only to upload the owner's videos.
  • Basic account info such as the account's display name and ID, used to confirm which account is connected.
  • The videos and captions the owner chooses to publish.

What it doesn't do

It doesn't read other users' data, messages, followers or analytics. It doesn't use cookies or tracking, show ads, or send data to any server other than TikTok's and Google's official APIs.

Google API Services

Clip District's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect at any time by deleting the stored tokens or by removing access in TikTok's settings (Security and permissions) or in their Google Account (Third-party connections).
